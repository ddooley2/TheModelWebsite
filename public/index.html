Methodology

Score-first

The system predicts each team’s final score. Projected spread and total are calculated from these scores.

Pre-kickoff only

Inputs use earlier game results, point-in-time opponent-adjusted ratings, preseason Elo, talent, recruiting, rest, week, neutral-site status, and other metrics to predict scores.

Market firewall

Opening and current lines are retrieved after predictions are created. They are displayed for comparison and never enter model fitting or score prediction. Our predictions cut through the betting hype.

Walk-forward evaluation

Each season is predicted by a model trained only on preceding seasons.
